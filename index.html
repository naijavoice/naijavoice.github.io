Our Purpose

Community problems should not disappear
into silence — they should rise.

NaijaVoice was built on a simple belief: when enough Nigerians speak together on the same issue, that issue becomes impossible to ignore. High-voted problems gain public visibility. Community-supported issues become harder to dismiss. We are not waiting for permission to be heard.

Founder's Note

"I built NaijaVoice because I got tired of watching real problems in Nigerian communities go unheard. Every road, every school, every water supply that fails — someone suffers. This platform exists so that suffering has a voice, and that voice has a number attached to it."

Abdullahi
Founder, NaijaVoice · Kogi State, Nigeria

Simple Process

How NaijaVoice Works

CASE 01
Post a Problem
Create a free account and describe a real issue affecting your community — with location, category and evidence.
CASE 02
Nigerians Vote
Anyone can vote on problems that affect them too. Every vote strengthens the signal.
CASE 03
Problems Rise
The more votes a problem gets, the higher it climbs — gaining public visibility and attention.
CASE 04
Issues Get Heard
High-voted, community-supported problems become harder to ignore by officials, media and NGOs.
