Design June 2026
A New Look for NaijaVoice
Every page on NaijaVoice has been redesigned with a new editorial identity — Fraunces, Inter and Geist Mono typography, a deep green and gold civic colour system, and a calmer, more confident visual language across the homepage, feed, profile, posting flow and problem pages.
New Pages June 2026
Trending & Leaderboard Pages Launched
Two new pages are now live:
  • Trending — see which problems are gaining votes fastest right now, separate from all-time top problems.
  • Leaderboard — top voters, top posters, most-supported citizens and the states leading the movement.
Feature June 2026
Threaded Replies & Comment Editing
Community discussions just got richer. You can now reply to a reply, edit your own comments and replies after posting, and follow conversations more naturally — Comment → Reply → Reply.
Improvement June 2026
Mobile Navigation Overhaul
A full mobile menu now gives instant access to every part of NaijaVoice — Problems, Search, Trending, Leaderboard, Updates, and posting — without needing to switch to desktop mode.
Fix June 2026
Image Uploads Now Display Correctly
Photos attached to community problems now appear properly on the feed and problem detail pages — giving every report stronger visual evidence and credibility.
Milestone March 2026
NaijaVoice Goes Live
NaijaVoice launched as a full civic platform — citizens can post community problems, vote on what matters most, and watch the highest-priority issues rise to the top. Built from scratch, for Nigeria.
