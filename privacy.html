🔒 Privacy

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it and how we protect it.

1. What Data We Collect

When you use NaijaVoice, we collect the following information:

📧
Email Address
Used for account creation, login and password reset
👤
Full Name
Displayed on your profile and posts
📍
State / Location
Used to categorise problems by region
📸
Uploaded Images
Photos you attach to problem reports
🗳️
Votes & Activity
Problems you posted, voted on and commented
📱
Device Info
Browser type and device used to access the platform

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information only for operating and improving NaijaVoice:

  • To create and manage your account
  • To display your posted problems and profile
  • To send account-related emails such as password resets
  • To improve platform features and user experience
  • To moderate content and ensure platform safety

🔒 We do not sell your personal data to any third party. Your information is used solely for operating the NaijaVoice civic platform.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a trusted cloud database provider. We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS)
  • Secure authentication and password hashing
  • Row-level security policies on all database tables
  • Regular security reviews and updates

4. Uploaded Images

Images you upload to document community problems are stored in secure cloud storage. Please note:

  • Images are publicly visible on problem posts
  • Do not upload images containing private personal information
  • Do not upload images of individuals without their consent
  • Images may be reviewed and removed if they violate guidelines

5. Your Rights

As a NaijaVoice user you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Update or correct your profile information at any time
  • Delete your posts and comments from the platform
  • Request account deletion by contacting us through the platform

6. Cookies & Technical Data

NaijaVoice uses minimal technical data to operate the platform. We use browser local storage to remember your login session and preferences. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes. Continued use of the platform after updates constitutes acceptance. Last updated: May 2026.
