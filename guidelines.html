📋 Platform Rules

Community Guidelines

NaijaVoice is a civic public-opinion and problem-reporting platform. These guidelines ensure we remain a safe, honest and responsible space for all Nigerians.

⚠️

Important: NaijaVoice is focused on community problems and locations — not targeting or attacking individuals. All posts must relate to civic, infrastructure or community issues affecting the public.

✅ What You Can Post

NaijaVoice welcomes civic reports that help communities and hold institutions accountable.

  • ✅
    Infrastructure problemsBad roads, broken bridges, power outages, water supply failures
  • ✅
    Public service failuresHospitals without equipment, schools without teachers, refuse not collected
  • ✅
    Environmental issuesFlooding, erosion, pollution affecting communities
  • ✅
    Community concernsSecurity issues, markets, public spaces, transportation
  • ✅
    Government accountabilityReporting unaddressed petitions, unfulfilled promises by institutions

🚫 What is Strictly Prohibited

The following content will be removed and may result in account suspension.

  • ❌
    False accusationsPosting claims you know to be untrue or unverified accusations targeting individuals
  • ❌
    Harassment & personal attacksUsing the platform to target, shame or intimidate specific individuals
  • ❌
    Hate speechContent targeting people based on ethnicity, religion, gender, tribe or origin
  • ❌
    Threats & incitementAny content that threatens violence or encourages illegal action
  • ❌
    DefamationFalse statements of fact that damage a person's reputation
  • ❌
    Private informationSharing someone's phone number, address, ID, bank details or personal documents
  • ❌
    Misleading edited evidenceDoctored images, screenshots or videos used to mislead
  • ❌
    Spam & irrelevant contentAdvertisements, promotions or content unrelated to civic issues
  • ❌
    Illegal contentContent that violates Nigerian law or promotes illegal activity

📸 Image Upload Guidelines

Images help document real problems. Please follow these image rules:

  • ✅
    Acceptable imagesRoads, flooding, electrical infrastructure, water problems, schools, hospitals, public infrastructure
  • ❌
    Not acceptableImages of individuals used for accusation, private documents, medical records, edited evidence

⚖️ Consequences of Violations

  • 🔴
    Content removalPosts violating guidelines will be removed without notice
  • 🔴
    Account suspensionRepeated violations lead to temporary or permanent account suspension
  • 🔴
    Legal liabilityUsers remain personally responsible for content they post under Nigerian law

🚩 How to Report Violations

See a post that violates these guidelines? Use the Report button on any post. Our moderation team will review it promptly. You can report for:

  • 🚩
    False information · Harassment · Hate speech · Privacy violation · Spam · Harmful content

Ready to Post Responsibly?

By posting on NaijaVoice you confirm that you have read and agreed to these Community Guidelines.
